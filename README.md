# Hump Day Hack

**A new IT case to crack, every Wednesday at 7am UK time.**
By Dean Baker, part of Walking IT.

▶ **Play it here: https://deanobaker3-alt.github.io/hump-day-hack/**

No account, no sign-in, no download. Open the link on any phone, tablet or computer and you're straight into this week's case.

## How it works

1. A ticket or alert lands on your desk: the kind a real service desk, network team or security analyst gets.
2. Two pieces of evidence are open. Three more are locked, and each one you open costs a clue.
3. Name what's actually wrong, then pick the fix. Three wrong turns and the case goes cold.
4. Share your result (it never gives the answer away). Every wrong answer you pick tells you why it's wrong.
5. When the next case drops, last week's moves to the archive with a full walkthrough.

Cases rotate through helpdesk, networking, Microsoft 365 and security.

## Privacy

Your progress and theme are saved in your own browser and never leave it. No accounts, no cookies, no tracking, no analytics.

## Made up on purpose

Every company, person, IP address and domain in the cases is invented: documentation IP ranges, example domains, fictional people. Commands and settings are real-world examples for learning, not instructions for any particular system.
